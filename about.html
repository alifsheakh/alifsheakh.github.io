About me

Md. Alif SheakhQLD · AU

I build machine learning and deep learning models that help clinicians diagnose disease from medical images and data, and explain how they reached their answer.

I hold a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from Daffodil International University (DIU), Bangladesh, and worked as a Research Associate at DIU’s Health Informatics Research Lab, collaborating with researchers at international universities on AI for medical diagnostics. My undergraduate thesis, MViTX, a multi-dataset vision transformer with explainable AI, received an A+ and was published in Neural Computing and Applications.

I have published more than 25 peer-reviewed papers across journals, conferences and a book chapter. I review for several international journals in AI and medical imaging. I also taught Algorithms and Machine Learning as a Teaching Assistant at DIU and mentored final-year students.

My current interests are graph neural networks, attention-based models and 3D data analysis for medical decision support.
