(02) Research

What I work on

My work sits where machine learning meets medicine: models that read medical images and clinical data accurately, explain their reasoning, and generalise across datasets.

01

Medical image analysis

New and optimised deep architectures for diagnosis from images: a multi-dataset vision transformer (MViTX), a gated MLP for endometrial cancer histopathology (ECgMLP), attention-based transfer learning for X-ray, hybrid transfer learning for brain tumours, and CNN ensembles for knee radiographs.

    Explainable AI

    Explainability is built into my models, not bolted on, so that clinicians can see which regions or features drove a decision. It runs through MViTX, FedVGM, the knee radiograph ensemble and breast cancer prediction.

      03

      Clinical machine learning

      Optimised machine-learning pipelines for disease prediction from clinical data, with feature selection, Bayesian and hyperparameter optimisation and ensembles. Applications include heart attack (ECG), liver disease, thyroid, hepatitis C, Parkinson’s, eye disease, and maternal and fetal health.

        Federated & robust learning
